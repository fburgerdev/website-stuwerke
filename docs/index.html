– of main dishes in Germany's university canteens are plant-based.

    Networks

    The Studierendenwerke coordinate through umbrella bodies. These are the ones this site has found a source for so far.

      Method

      Plant-based means labelled vegan. A dish counts as plant-based when the canteen labels it vegan, in its notes, its category or its name. Vegetarian dishes are counted separately.

      Where the numbers come from. Menus are read automatically, without any AI. Most come from OpenMensa, a volunteer-run open database of canteen menus, read every day. Where that has no working feed, the menus are read from the Studierendenwerk's own website or API, as often as it publishes ahead: a site that posts one week in advance is read once a week. Student numbers, cities and managing directors come from the Deutsches Studierendenwerk's yearly statistics (Zahlenspiegel 2024/2025).

      What counts. Only main dishes. Sides, desserts, salad bars and anything under €1.30 for students are left out. Shares cover the last four weeks.

      What this does not show. These are dishes offered, not dishes sold. A canteen with many plant-based options can still sell mostly meat. Labels are the canteens' own and sometimes wrong or missing, so every figure is an estimate.

      Missing data. A dashed circle means no menu data yet, not that nothing is happening there. Where a canteen labels few of its dishes, the plant-based share is a lower bound and the circle is shown faded.

      Averages. State and national figures weight each Studierendenwerk by its student numbers. Only those with well-labelled menus enter the averages; the faded ones are shown but left out, because a lower bound would pull the average down.

      Highlights. Diamonds on the map mark things worth knowing: a fully plant-based canteen, a decision, a trial. Each has a source. A canteen whose whole menu was plant-based over the last four weeks is marked automatically.

      PETA ranking. Where PETA Deutschland rated a canteen in its 2026 canteen ranking, the points appear in the Studierendenwerk's details. That score comes from a questionnaire the Studierendenwerke fill in and covers one canteen each, so it measures something different from the menu share shown here.

      People. Names appear only where the organisation or its association publishes them, with the source linked. This site makes no judgement about individuals.

      Corrections. Something wrong or missing? Report it here. Code and data are open.
