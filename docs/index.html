    Method

    Where the numbers come from. Menus are read every day from OpenMensa, a volunteer-run open database of canteen menus. Student numbers, cities and managing directors come from the Deutsches Studierendenwerk's yearly statistics (Zahlenspiegel 2024/2025).

    What counts. Only main dishes are counted. Sides, desserts, salad bars and anything under €1.30 for students are left out. A dish counts as vegan or vegetarian when the canteen labels it that way, in its notes, its category or its name. Shares are over the last four weeks.

    What this does not show. These are dishes offered, not dishes sold. A canteen with many vegan options can still sell mostly meat. Labels are the canteens' own and sometimes wrong or missing, so every figure is an estimate.

    Missing data. A hollow circle means no open menu data, not that nothing is happening there. Where a canteen labels few of its dishes, the vegan share is a lower bound and the circle is shown faded.

    Averages. State and national figures weight each Studierendenwerk by its student numbers, so a large one counts for more than a small one. Only Studierendenwerke with well-labelled menus enter the averages; the faded ones are shown but left out, because a lower bound would pull the average down.

    People. Names appear only where the organisation or its association publishes them, with the source linked. This site makes no judgement about individuals.

    Corrections. Something wrong or missing? Report it here. Code and data are open.
